# Rantau Domain Context

## Opportunity

An employer, program, or verified pathway that a person can pursue. An Opportunity can be a job, Ausbildung, internship, apprenticeship, or vocational placement.

## Direct Apply

The authoritative destination where a person can take the next application step. It should point to the employer or program owner when available.

## Readiness Plan

The ordered preparation needed before Direct Apply, including documents, language level, licenses, visa steps, and trusted support institutions.

## Search Intent

What a person describes in natural language about role, place, work mode, pathway, experience, and constraints.

## Candidate Profile

The person-owned facts used for personalization, including skills, languages, experience, education, preferred places, and pathway preferences.

## Application

A person-owned record that tracks one Opportunity from saved through applied, interview, offer, accepted, or rejected.

## Source Evidence

The URL, publisher, retrieval time, and extracted facts that support an Opportunity and its Readiness Plan.

## Cost Envelope

The monthly operating estimate for Convex, AI Gateway, Firecrawl, and AgentMail at a stated usage level.
